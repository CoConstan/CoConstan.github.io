I am a PhD student at [CREATIS](http://www.creatis.insa-lyon.fr/), supervised by [David SARRUT](https://calm-sundae-1df120.netlify.app/) and [Ane ETXEBESTE](https://www.creatis.insa-lyon.fr/~etxebeste/). I am part of the ‘[Tomoradio](https://www.creatis.insa-lyon.fr/site7/en/tomoradio)’ team, which focuses on tomographic imaging and radiation therapy.

My research focuses on SPECT reconstruction algorithms, mainly in the context of Lu-177 therapy, with particular emphasis on unrolled neural networks and optimization methods.
